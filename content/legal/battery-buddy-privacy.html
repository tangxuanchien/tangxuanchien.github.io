Privacy Policy

Battery Buddy: Emoji Battery · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Battery Buddy: Emoji Battery app (package com.txclabs.emoji.battery, "the App"), published by TXC Labs ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. The App has no accounts, does not show ads, and offers optional Premium subscriptions sold through Google Play.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or payment information. Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your payment details, only whether a subscription is active. The App does not show ads or use advertising identifiers for advertising. Firebase Analytics, described below, may collect the Android Advertising ID for analytics when it is available on your device.

2. Information processed on your device only

  • Your customizations – chosen battery icons, stickers, status bar, notch and mascot settings, bookmarks and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Images you add – photos or GIFs you pick to use as stickers, icons or backgrounds are copied into the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Installed app list – if you use the "exclude apps" feature, the App reads the list of launchable apps on your device so you can choose where overlays should be hidden. This list stays on your device.

3. Accessibility Service

The App offers an optional Accessibility Service, which you must turn on yourself in Android Settings. It is used solely to draw the custom status bar, battery icon, notch and stickers on top of other apps, and to hide them in apps you have excluded. To do that it only receives the name of the app currently in the foreground. It cannot read screen content (window content retrieval is disabled), does not record what you type or view, and nothing it receives is stored or sent off your device. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

4. Other permissions

  • Display over other apps – to show the animated mascot (shimeji) on your screen.
  • Notifications / foreground service – to keep the mascot overlay running and let you stop it from the notification.
  • Internet – to download catalogs of icons, stickers and mascots.

5. Information collected automatically

The App includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These SDKs transmit the data described below to Google over HTTPS so we can understand app usage and diagnose crashes:

  • Firebase Analytics – app interactions, including the screen events the App logs, app version, device model and operating system, language, a pseudonymous app-installation identifier, and the Android Advertising ID when available. Google Analytics may also derive coarse location such as country from the network address used to deliver requests. We use these data for usage measurement and product improvement, not to show or target ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash stack traces, relevant app and device state at the time of a crash, app and device technical information, and a pseudonymous Crashlytics installation identifier. Because Analytics is also included, crash reports may include Analytics event breadcrumbs that describe recent in-app actions.

These identifiers relate to an app installation and are not used by us to identify you by name. Google processes the data under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Firebase states that Analytics data retention depends on the project settings; Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before deletion begins.

When the App downloads content (icons, stickers, mascots) from our content delivery network (Bunny.net), the CDN receives your IP address and standard request information as part of normal internet traffic. It is used only to deliver the files.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Other than the service providers described above, we do not share information with third parties, except where required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Analytics data is retained according to Firebase project settings (which can be set for up to 14 months). Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. You can request deletion of data associated with your app installation by contacting us at the address below.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Network requests are made over HTTPS where supported. While no method is 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to protect the limited data we process.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this policy, contact us at tangxuanchien511@gmail.com.
