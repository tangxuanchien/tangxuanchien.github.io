Privacy Policy

Domi: Photobooth Camera · com.txclabs.photobooth
Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Domi: Photobooth Camera ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we process

  • Camera & photos. The App uses your device camera to take photos and lets you decorate them with frames, stickers, filters and fonts. Photos are processed on your device and are saved to your device (Pictures/Domi Photobooth) only when you tap Save. We do not upload your photos to our servers. You can delete saved photos at any time from the Library tab or your gallery.
  • Photos from your gallery. If you choose to use existing photos, the App uses the Android photo picker and only receives the photos you select. If you tap Share, the finished photo is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet.
  • Downloaded content. Frames, stickers, fonts and the catalog that lists them are downloaded from Cloudinary (cloudinary.com), a third-party image hosting and delivery service, so content can be updated without an app update. Like any web request, this makes your IP address and basic request details (such as the app's user agent) visible to Cloudinary. We do not send it any personal data about you or your photos.
  • Usage & diagnostics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage. This may include a device identifier, device model, OS version, language, app version and in-app events. It is used only in aggregate to improve the App.
  • Advertising. The free version of the App shows ads (banner, native, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Premium subscribers do not see ads.
  • Premium purchases. Subscriptions are bought and billed through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; the App only receives confirmation from Google Play of which subscription is active, so it can unlock Premium features.
  • App configuration. The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings (such as which ads to show) without an app update. This uses a Firebase installation identifier and basic device and app information.
  • Stored on your device. Settings such as your language choice, your ad consent choice, your Premium status and a cached copy of the catalog are kept locally on your device and are not sent to us.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not collect your contacts, location or browsing history.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following services. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies:

  • Cloudinary (content delivery): cloudinary.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Remote Config (usage statistics, app configuration): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (ads and ad consent): how Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions): Google Payments privacy notice

4. Permissions

  • Camera — to capture photos in the photobooth.
  • Internet and network state — to download the frame / sticker / font catalog, load ads and check subscriptions.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID and Android ad services) — used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads, as described above.
  • Google Play Billing — to buy and restore Premium subscriptions.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — to save finished photos to your gallery. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Data retention

Photos you save stay on your device until you delete them (in the Library tab or in your gallery). Aggregated analytics are retained per Google Firebase's default retention settings. Advertising data is retained by Google as described in its policies.

7. Your rights

You can clear app data or uninstall the App at any time to stop all data processing. You may contact us to request deletion of any diagnostic data linked to your device identifier.

See also our Terms of Use.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
