About this appVề ứng dụng

BitArcade is a pocket arcade for classic handheld games. Load your own GBA, GB and GBC game files, play with responsive on-screen controls and pick up exactly where you left off with save states. No games are included.BitArcade là máy chơi game cầm tay bỏ túi cho các game cổ điển.
